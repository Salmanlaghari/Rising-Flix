Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Rising Flix ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Premium Video Streaming application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure streaming environment.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal identifiable information (PII) from our users. All content links are fetched dynamically from the public domain or designated JSON source configurations.

2. App Permissions

We only request permissions that are absolutely critical to the core functioning of the application:

  • INTERNET: To stream movies, dramas, and sports from public links.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To monitor and auto-retry video streaming on reconnection.

3. Offline Storage

Your downloaded video files are stored locally on your device storage and are completely managed by you. We do not transmit any stored files to external servers.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to our team at support@risingflix.com.
